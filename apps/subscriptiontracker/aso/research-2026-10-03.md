# ASO research — Nikatru Subscription Tracker, 2026-10-03

Lane aso-listings (club-rt-store-b). Row O-ASO-RESEARCH-NEVER-RUN. No paid tool, no search-volume data and
no private API: every claim below is either the app's own register or a public listing page, with its URL and
the date it was read. Competitors' prices were not read and are not recorded.

## 1. Method

1. **The jobs users search for**, taken from what the app actually does — `apps/subscriptiontracker/dod.json`
   `features` (home, calendar, insights, budget, import, detail, add, cancel), `app.yaml` (`tagline: Track every
   subscription in one place`, `ai.generatesContent: false`) and the shipped strings in `lib/l10n/app_{en,hi,ta}.arb`.
2. **Competitors' public listing pages** (section 3): title, subtitle and first sentences, read on 2026-10-03.
3. **Per channel and locale, the chosen fields** (section 4), each held by `tooling/ci/assert-store-listings.mjs`
   to the store's sourced limit (`tooling/channel-register.json` `maxChars` / `maxLines` / `maxBytes`) and to the
   claims rules (no commission, no web price, no AI, no competitor name in Apple keywords).

Stated limit: without a keyword-volume tool the ranking of candidates is by relevance to the app and by what
competitors' titles reveal people search for, not by measured traffic. A volume pass is a later lane's, and is
a spend decision.

The Play pages themselves were truncated when fetched, so the Play rows record the TITLE (exact, from the
search result) and what the public search index says of the page, not a verbatim description.

**What the read shows.**
1. Every title is built from the same three words — *subscription*, *tracker*, *reminder/manager*. Ours already
   carries the first two in the App Name, so on Apple they are indexed from the title and **repeating them in the
   keywords field wastes bytes**.
2. Renewal reminders are the lead promise of most listings; ours makes it too, so it is not a differentiator.
3. **No listing read leads with "no bank login".** Ours does in the body ("It does not ask for access to your bank
   account"); a custom listing can put it first (section 4, custom listings).
4. **None of the App Store competitors read lists Hindi or Tamil.** A reviewed hi/ta listing is uncontested
   copy in India's two largest non-English markets this app ships.

## 4. Chosen fields, per channel and locale

English (the flat tree, the register's `sourceLocale`). Unchanged except the Apple keywords:

| Channel | Field | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| all | title | Nikatru Subscription Tracker | the product name (`app.yaml`), 29 of 30 |
| android-play, windows-store, … | short description | Track every subscription in one place | the tagline; the job in the user's words |
| ios-appstore, macos-appstore | subtitle | Every subscription, one list | 28 of 30 |
| ios-appstore, macos-appstore | keywords | `recurring,renewal,reminder,bills,spending,budget,expenses,payments,monthly,costs,manager,due` | **changed**: drops `subscription,tracker` (already indexed from the App Name, §3.1) and spends the bytes on the jobs in §2; 92 of 100 bytes |
| ios-appstore, macos-appstore | promotional text | as shipped, and then the calendar in `aso/promo-calendar.json` | the one field that changes without a release |
| windows-store | search terms | as shipped (7 of 7) | |

Hindi and Tamil: `aso/pending/hi.json` and `aso/pending/ta.json`, agent-drafted in this session (no translation
API, ADR 033) with an English back-translation per field, using the app's own strings (सदस्यता, नवीनीकरण;
சந்தா, புதுப்பிப்பு). The title stays the product name, as the app's `appTitle` does in both ARBs. Keywords:
`सदस्यता,नवीनीकरण,रिमाइंडर,बिल,ख़र्च` (97 of 100 bytes) and `சந்தா,புதுப்பிப்பு,நினைவூட்டல்,பில்` (99 of 100
bytes) — Apple's limit is in BYTES, three per Devanagari or Tamil character, so a Tamil keyword field holds four
words where English holds twelve. They are **pending**: the review sheets `aso/review/{hi,ta}.tsv` await an
independent checker, and the owner rows on the exemptions (O-ST-POLISH-P3, O-TAMIL-NOTICE-REVIEW) still hold.

Not localised, by the table `tooling/store/listing-languages.json`: linux-snap (one summary and description) and
apps-gov-in (one App Name and Description field).

Custom listings (`aso/custom-listings.json`): a Play country-targeted custom store listing for India that leads
with "No bank login, no card number" (§3.3), and an Apple custom product page for renewal-reminder traffic.
Neither exists in a console: creating them is a lead step and publishing is the owner's.
