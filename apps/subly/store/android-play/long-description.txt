Subly keeps every subscription you pay for in one list, so the renewal that
would have surprised you next month is a thing you already knew about.

Add each service once with its price and billing cycle. Subly works out what
you spend per month and per year, shows the next payment date for each one,
and reminds you before a free trial turns into a charge.

WHAT IT DOES
- One list of every subscription, with price, currency and billing cycle.
- Monthly and yearly totals, recalculated as you edit.
- Next-renewal dates, sorted so the nearest is first.
- Reminders before a renewal or the end of a free trial.
- Works offline. Your list is stored on your device and stays readable
  without a connection.

WHY IT IS DIFFERENT
Subly does not ask for access to your bank account and does not read your
transactions. There is no bank login, no card number, no read-only connection
to a financial institution. You tell it what you subscribe to; it does the
arithmetic and the remembering. That is the whole product.

PERMISSIONS
Subly asks for notification permission so it can remind you before a renewal
or the end of a trial. Decline it and every other feature still works — you
just do not get reminders.

PRIVACY
Subly does not sell your data and does not show ads. Analytics are off until
you turn them on, and you can delete your account and its data from inside
the app. Full policy: https://nikatru.com/privacy
