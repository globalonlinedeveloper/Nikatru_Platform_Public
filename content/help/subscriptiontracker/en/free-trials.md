  - How do I track a free trial?

  - How do I enter the price after the trial?
  - Can I see which subscriptions are on trial?
  - How do I avoid being charged after a free trial?
---
When you add or edit a subscription, turn on **Free trial** and set when the **trial ends** and the price **then** (after the trial). The board shows "Free trial until …, then …", and you get a **Free trial ending** reminder before the first charge.

Use the **Trial** filter on the board to see every subscription on a trial.
