**Insights** shows your spend per month or per year, the next 30 days of charges, your biggest subscription, and spend by category as bars or a table.

**Worth a look** points out things to check: several plans in the same category, a large yearly charge coming soon, and plans you may not use. "Still using it?" is a question — the tracker cannot see your usage, so it asks.
