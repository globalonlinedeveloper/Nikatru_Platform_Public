Delete your account in the app (**Settings**, Account & data) or on the [delete account page](https://nikatru.com/delete-account). Your account and its data are deleted, including your problem reports.

Deleting your account does not cancel a purchase made in the App Store or Google Play: manage it in that store's subscription settings. See [pricing](https://nikatru.com/pricing) and the [refund policy](https://nikatru.com/refund) for plans.
