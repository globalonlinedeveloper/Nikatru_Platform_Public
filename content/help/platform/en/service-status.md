The [status page](https://status.nikatru.com/) lists each Nikatru service — the websites, the apps, sign-in and sync — and whether it is working. It is updated when a service changes state, and at least once a day.

A service our checks cannot see yet is shown as "not monitored", never as working. Past problems, what broke and what we did about them, are listed under incident history.

If the status page says everything is working but something is wrong for you, [report a problem](https://nikatru.com/support#report-a-problem).
