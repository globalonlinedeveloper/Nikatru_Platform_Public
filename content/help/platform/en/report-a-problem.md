Use the [Report a problem](https://nikatru.com/support#report-a-problem) form on the support page. Choose what kind of problem it is (choose **A question** to ask anything), describe it, and send. You do not need an account.

Tick "you may reply to me" and give your email address if you want an answer; we reply within 2 business days. Your report goes to our private support system, never to a public page.

From an app, choose **Report a problem** under Help in Settings; from FullShot, use **Open the report form** in its options.
