Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we are asked most often. Open a question to read the answer.

How do I reset my password?

Use the reset link on the sign-in screen. The link is valid for one hour.

How do I contact support?

Write to rajasekar@nikatru.com and quote your reference number. We answer within one working day.

Where can I find my invoices?

Invoices are under Billing in the account menu. They are kept for seven years.

Still stuck?
