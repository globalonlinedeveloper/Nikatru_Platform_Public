Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026 (version 2026-10-04)

This Privacy Policy explains how Nikatru ("Nikatru", "we", "us", or "our"), a proprietorship of Rajasekar Selvam based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India, handles information in connection with our website nikatru.com and our applications and games published across iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, Linux and the web (together, the "Services").

This Policy is a notice: it tells you what we do with personal data. It is not something you agree to by using the Services. Where we rely on your consent, we ask for it separately, by a clear action of yours, and you can withdraw it as easily as you gave it.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us — for example, your email address when you contact support, send feedback, or (in apps that offer accounts) create an account.
  • Problem reports — if you use "Report a problem" in an app, on this site or in an extension, we store what you write, the category you chose, a screenshot only if you attach one (text fields and anything marked private are blurred on your device before it is sent), and the basic diagnostics shown to you before you send (app version, platform, device type, language and recent error codes; logs only if you tick that box). We keep your email address with a report only if you ask us to reply or to tell you when it is fixed, and we mail you only about that report: a receipt if you asked for a reply, and one note when it is fixed if you asked for that (each mail has an unsubscribe link). We delete each report and its screenshot after 90 days, and deleting your account deletes your reports.
  • Information collected automatically — basic technical data such as device model, operating system and version, app version, language, approximate region and crash/diagnostic logs, which help us fix bugs and keep the Services working.
  • Usage statistics, only if you allow them — if you agree when asked, we record which features of an app are used and when, so we can see what is working and what is not. These records are pseudonymous, not anonymous: each one carries a random identifier created on your device the first time the app runs, and that identifier stays the same until the app is uninstalled. It is not your name, your email or an advertising ID, and we cannot use it to identify you — but because it is stable, records from the same installation can be grouped together, which is why we describe it as pseudonymous rather than anonymous. We do not collect or store your IP address with these records; an approximate location (country, region, city) is determined at our network edge and the IP address itself is discarded. You can turn usage statistics off at any time in the app's Settings, and turning them off stops collection from that moment.
  • Launch-notification signups — if you ask us to tell you when an app launches, we store your email address and the time you signed up, and nothing else. To stop that form being abused we also keep a short-lived counter keyed to a salted, one-way fingerprint of the network address the request came from. The fingerprint is not reversible to an IP address, it is never stored alongside your email, and it is discarded automatically within an hour.
  • Network addresses — apart from a pending sign-in verification (below), we do not keep the network address a request comes from with your account or with any record about you. Two things see it, and neither keeps it with your data: Cloudflare, at our network edge, uses it to deliver the request, to stop abuse and to work out an approximate location, and then discards it, apart from Cloudflare's own security logs; and the request log of our own sign-in service records it for security and fault diagnosis and deletes it after 7 days. Our sign-in service records the address on each sign-in, and since 2 October 2026 the database it writes to empties that field on every write, with one exception: a pending sign-in verification briefly holds the network address it was requested from. If you use a second sign-in step, its one-time code is accepted only from the address that asked for it, so that address is kept until the code is used; it is erased when the verification succeeds, or, if it is never completed, within a day of the code expiring, 5 minutes after it was requested. Copies made before that date remain until they age out: a sign-in session opened before 2 October 2026 keeps the address it was opened from until it next refreshes, which happens whenever the app is used; the encrypted backups of the sign-in records taken before that date contain those addresses; and so does the fallback copy Supabase holds (section 4).
  • Purchase information — if you buy something, the transaction is processed by the relevant app store or by our payment processor. We receive confirmation of the purchase but do not collect or store your full card or bank details.

We do not sell your personal information.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, operate, maintain and improve the Services;
  • To diagnose crashes and technical problems and enhance performance;
  • To respond to your support requests and communicate with you;
  • To process purchases and provide receipts; and
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud or abuse.

3. Offers and promotions inside our apps

Some of our apps show occasional messages about our own products — a paid upgrade to the app you are already using, or another Nikatru app. These are first-party promotions: we write them, we show them ourselves, and no advertising network, ad exchange or advertising SDK is involved. Nobody pays us to show them to you.

The legal basis we rely on. Where the law where you live requires us to state one, we rely on our legitimate interest in promoting our own products to the people who already use them (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). The interest is narrow and we would rather name it than gesture at it: telling someone who already uses one of our apps that a paid version exists, or that the same small publisher makes another app, is how a business with no advertising budget reaches the people it already serves. We weighed that against your interests. These messages appear only inside an app you chose to open, they are chosen from what you have already paid for and from nothing else, and you can switch them off in one tap.

What we use to decide what to show you. Your entitlement state — whether you have a paid plan for the app you are using — and nothing else. That decision is made on your device. We do not build a profile of you, we do not use your behaviour inside the app to choose these messages, we do not use any advertising identifier, and we never look at which other apps are installed on your device.

Your right to object, and how to use it. You can object at any time to your information being used for direct marketing, and that right is absolute: if you object, we stop. There is nothing for us to weigh and no reason we could give for carrying on (GDPR Article 21(2)). You do not have to write to us. Open the app's Settings and turn Offers and promotions off — promotions stop immediately, and they stay off, because we record that you objected so that nothing can turn them back on. You can also object by emailing support@nikatru.com. Objecting costs you nothing else: no feature is removed, no content is withheld, and nothing you have paid for changes.

These messages are about Nikatru apps only. We do not promote anyone else's products, we are not paid to show you anything, and we do not share any information with anyone else in order to show them.

Offer codes and inviting a friend. If you redeem an offer code, we keep a record that your account redeemed it, which offer it was and when the free months end; the code itself is kept only in a scrambled form that cannot be read back. If you invite a friend, or accept a friend's invitation, we keep a record of who invited whom — the two accounts, the app, and whether the reward was given — so that each invited person is rewarded once and nobody is rewarded more than five times a year. Before the reward, we check only that the invited account is at least 7 days old, has a confirmed email address, has started using the app and has never had a paid plan or a trial of it. The record is deleted when the invited person deletes their account; if the person who invited them deletes theirs, their name is taken off it. Free months never include the AI features.

4. Third-party services

We rely on trusted third parties to deliver the Services. Who takes your payment, and who is the seller, depends on where you buy:

  • In an app from the Apple App Store or Google Play, the store bills the purchase through its own system and sells it under its own terms. RevenueCat records what that purchase entitles you to, so an app knows what you have paid for; it processes that on our behalf and is never the seller.
  • Everywhere else we sell — this website, the Microsoft Store, the Snap Store, our Linux download (AppImage), the Chrome Web Store, Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Firefox Add-ons — outside India, the seller is Paddle, acting as our merchant of record: Paddle takes the payment, issues the receipt and handles sales tax.
  • When you buy in India anywhere other than the Apple App Store or Google Play, Nikatru is the seller and Razorpay takes the payment as our payment gateway, processing your payment details on our behalf.

We also rely on Cloudflare (hosting, and the network edge our website and endpoints run on); Hostinger, which rents us the servers our own sign-in service runs on, so it holds the account credentials and sign-in sessions for apps that offer accounts; Supabase, which ran that sign-in service until 27 September 2026 and still holds a fallback copy of the account and sign-in records from before that date until it is retired; Resend, which delivers the email we send you (sign-in and account-verification messages, and the renewal reminders you choose to receive, which list the subscriptions about to renew) and therefore processes your email address on our behalf; and Google, whose Google Workspace hosts our support mailbox, so it holds what you write to us, and whose Google Drive stores our backups, which contain a copy of the account and app records described here. Where an app offers them, Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google are optional ways to sign in: if you choose one, Apple or Google shares with us the email address and name on that account, as you allow, and you can change the name in the app's Settings. We do not sell anything directly from this website today; the arrangements above are the ones that apply when we do. Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review the policies of the app store you use.

We do not use advertising networks or advertising SDKs, we do not sell or share data with data brokers or advertisers, and no Nikatru app carries third-party advertising. The only promotional messages in our apps are our own, about our own products; section 3 explains how they work and how to turn them off. Usage statistics and crash reports are likewise not sent to a third-party analytics product: they are received and stored by software we run ourselves. Usage statistics run on infrastructure provided by Cloudflare as our hosting provider; crash reports are received by a server we operate ourselves on Hostinger.

5. Sharing of information

We share information only as needed to operate the Services (for example, with the payment processor to complete a purchase), to comply with the law or a valid legal request, or to protect our rights and the safety of users. We do not sell or rent personal data to third parties.

6. Data retention

We keep information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described here or as required by law. You can ask us to delete information associated with you by emailing us (see Contact).

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Age requirement

Our Services are for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to use the Services or to create an account, and no Nikatru app, extension or site is designed for or directed to anyone younger. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 treats every person under 18 as a child, and 18 is the single floor we apply everywhere we operate rather than a different age per country.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights, and how to complain

You have the right to get a copy of the personal data we hold about you and a summary of how we use it; to have it corrected or completed; to have it erased; to withdraw a consent you gave; to nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act yourself; and to complain, to us and then to the Data Protection Board of India. The law where you live may give you more, and we honour that too.

How to use them. In an app, open Settings, then Your privacy rights: there you can download a copy of everything our servers hold about your account at once, name your nominee, and delete your account. Anyone, signed in or not, can also send a request at nikatru.com/privacy-rights or email support@nikatru.com. When you ask to use a privacy right, we keep the request, what you wrote and the address our answer goes to, for 400 days after we close it, as the record that we answered. A request sent without signing in counts only once you confirm it from the email we send, so nobody can make one in your name. We acknowledge every request within 48 hours and resolve it within 30 days.

Your nominee. In the app you can name one person, by their name and email address, to use your rights if you die or become unable to act. We tell them nothing until they contact us, and deleting your account deletes the nomination.

Usage statistics can be withdrawn without contacting us. Open the app's Settings and turn Usage statistics off; collection stops immediately. We keep a record of each consent decision (that a decision was made, when, and which version of this Policy you were shown) so that we can demonstrate the choice was yours. That record contains no IP address and no device fingerprint.

How to complain

Write to our Grievance Officer, Rajasekar Selvam, Proprietor, at support@nikatru.com (other ways to reach us are on the Contact page). We acknowledge a complaint within 48 hours and resolve it within 30 days. If we do not resolve it, or you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

10. International users

We operate from India, and information may be processed in India and in countries where our service providers operate.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version here with a new "Last updated" date, and every earlier version stays available. If a change affects something you consented to, we ask you again in the app before the change applies to your data.

12. Cookies and local storage

This website sets no cookies. One page keeps one thing in your browser: when you choose an app on the Pricing page, the page stores that app’s name in your browser’s local storage so that the checkout opens for the right app. It says nothing about you and never leaves your browser.

Our web apps set no cookies either. Like an installed app, a web app keeps its working data in your browser’s local storage, on your device: your sign-in session, your settings and choices (such as language, theme and the consent choices you made), a random install identifier (used with usage statistics only if you allow them), anything waiting to be sent while you are offline, and a copy of your own app data so that the app works offline. Signing out ends the session, and you can clear the rest in your browser’s settings.
