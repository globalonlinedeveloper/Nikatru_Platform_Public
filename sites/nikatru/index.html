The pledge

Six commitments, each with the thing that would prove it broken.

We have no users to quote and no logos to show you, and we are not going to invent either. This is the alternative: specific promises, in writing, that you can check yourself.

  • No advertising, and no third-party tracking, in any Nikatru app

    No ad SDK, no analytics broker, no data sold or brokered to anyone, ever.

    Check it The privacy policy lists every category of data each app collects and why. Any ad identifier in a shipped build breaks this.

  • Your data can be deleted, by you, without asking us

    Account deletion is a page on this site, not an email you have to send and chase.

    Check it Delete your account works today, for the live product, whether or not you ever paid us.

  • We do not claim a platform we are not on

    The channel marks above are generated from the release register. Nothing on this site can advertise a store the product has not shipped to.

    Check it Every store name on this site is checked against the published listings on every build. A false badge fails the build, not a review.

  • Prices are shown before checkout, with the renewal terms beside them

    What it costs, what it renews at, and when — on the same screen as the button.

    Check it Pricing and the refund policy are public and dated. Paid checkout is not open yet; nothing here can be bought today.

  • Support is answered by the person who wrote the code

    One developer, one inbox. No ticket deflection, no chatbot in front of it.

    Check it Support and contact both reach the same address, published in full.

  • Every policy change is kept, dated, and readable after the fact

    The version you agreed to stays available; it is not quietly overwritten.

    Check it Privacy and terms carry an effective date, and earlier versions are archived rather than replaced.

Who builds it

One person, named, with an address you can write to.

Nikatru is the studio of Rajasekar Selvam, a Chennai-based developer with eleven years in software. It started in 2021 with one goal: make well-crafted, privacy-respecting apps that work on every screen — and stand behind them afterwards, which is the part most software skips.

It is registered as an MSME in Tamil Nadu under UDYAM-TN-02-0487004. That number is on every page of this site because a company you can look up is a company you can hold to something. If an app of ours costs you money or loses your data, there is exactly one person to answer for it, and he is on this page.
