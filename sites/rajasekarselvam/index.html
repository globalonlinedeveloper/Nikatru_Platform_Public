About

I'm an independent software developer who takes products end to end — design, development, testing and publishing — on my own. I build with Flutter, so one codebase can serve phones, desktops and the web.

I work under my studio brand Nikatru, a Government of India registered MSME (Udyam UDYAM-TN-02-0487004) established in 2021. I care about clean design, performance and shipping things that actually reach people.

My background is professional software development, spanning enterprise systems and independent products; I now put that experience entirely into designing, building and shipping under Nikatru.
